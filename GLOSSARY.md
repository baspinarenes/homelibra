# Homelibra

Homelibra is a research-notes library for historians and other humanities scholars. Notes taken while reading or watching a source stay anchored to where they came from and carry tags, so that everything relevant to a topic can be pulled together when the scholar sits down to write.

## Language

### Library

**Library** (Kütüphane):
Everything one user has recorded: their sources, notes, tags and collections. Private to that user.
_Avoid_: workspace, account, archive, vault

**Source** (Kaynak):
A work, or a body of records, that the scholar works through and takes notes on: a book, an article, a thesis, an archive fond or a single document, a manuscript, a newspaper run, a recording or a web page. What counts as one source is the scholar's choice; every source has exactly one source type.
_Avoid_: book, reference, material, item, document

**Source type** (Kaynak türü):
One of a fixed list of kinds a source belongs to, chosen by what identifies the work and how places inside it are cited, not by genre. The list is under _Source types_ below.
_Avoid_: category, format, medium, genre

**Date** (Tarih):
When a source was published, written or issued, recorded in the calendar the source itself uses (Milâdî, Hicrî or Rumî) and to the precision the scholar knows: the year, the month or the day. For a printed work it is the date of the edition in hand, the one its locators refer to; a first edition is recorded separately.
_Avoid_: year, timestamp, publication year

**Bibliographic note** (Künye notu):
Free-form bibliographic detail about a source that fits none of its fields: a place of publication, a translator, a series, a fond's full name. Never the scholar's thoughts; those belong in a comment.
_Avoid_: description, notes, remarks, summary

**Reading status** (Okuma durumu):
Whether a source is still being read or finished. Nothing more: no dates, ratings or progress.
_Avoid_: progress, shelf, state

### Source types

**Book** (Kitap):
A monograph, an edited volume or an e-book, cited by page.

**Article** (Makale):
A journal article, a chapter in an edited volume or an encyclopaedia entry, cited by page within the work that contains it.
_Avoid_: paper, essay, chapter, entry

**Thesis** (Tez):
A master's or doctoral thesis, cited by page.
_Avoid_: dissertation

**Archive** (Arşiv):
An archive fond, a single document or a register (defter) worked through as one source, cited by document reference, folio or enclosure.
_Avoid_: archive document, record, file

**Manuscript** (Yazma):
A handwritten work held in a library collection, cited by folio.
_Avoid_: codex, handwritten book

**Periodical** (Süreli yayın):
A newspaper or magazine run worked through as one source, cited by issue and page.
_Avoid_: newspaper, magazine, journal

**Recording** (Kayıt):
A documentary, podcast, lecture video or interview recording, cited by time.
_Avoid_: video, audio, film, episode

**Web page** (Web sayfası):
Online content that is none of the above, usually cited without a locator.
_Avoid_: link, site, URL

**Other** (Diğer):
Anything else the scholar takes notes on: a map, an image, a live lecture, an exhibition.

### Notes

**Note** (Not):
A single record anchored to one locator of one source, holding an excerpt, a comment, or both.
_Avoid_: highlight, annotation, card, entry, text

**Excerpt** (Alıntı):
The author's own words, copied verbatim, inside a note.
_Avoid_: quote, highlight, passage, text

**Comment** (Yorum):
The scholar's own words about a passage or a source, inside a note.
_Avoid_: note, thought, annotation, remark

**Locator** (Konum):
Where in a source a note comes from, written as the scholar would cite it: a page, a page range, a timestamp, a folio, a document reference, an issue and page. Free-form; the notes of a source are listed in its order, and a note with no locator speaks of the source as a whole.
_Avoid_: page, position, reference, citation

### Finding things again

**Tag** (Etiket):
A flat, free-form label attached to notes and to sources. Tags have no hierarchy and no type.
_Avoid_: topic, keyword, category, subject, label

**Collection** (Dosya):
A saved, named view that gathers every note carrying any of a chosen set of tags, typically for a piece of writing in progress. Notes are not moved into a collection; it is a lens over the library.
_Avoid_: project, folder, notebook, topic, saved search

### Workflow

**Capture** (Yakalama):
Recording a note quickly while reading or watching, usually on the phone, possibly without tags.
_Avoid_: entry, input, logging

**Organizing pass** (Düzenleme turu):
Going through a finished source's notes to tag them and tidy them up.
_Avoid_: review, triage, cleanup
